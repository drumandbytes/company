Privacy

This page covers drumandbytes.ee only. Each app and project has its own policy on its own site.

What this website collects

drumandbytes.ee is a single static page. There are no accounts, forms, cookies or tracking scripts of ours. Cloudflare sits in front of the site and keeps two kinds of aggregate counts:

  • Page views (Cloudflare Web Analytics): which pages get visited, roughly how many times, and referrers.
  • Referrals: which site or link sent a visit, meaning the referring site's name (or a short tag we add to our own links) and the page path, without the query string. Kept for three months.

Neither sets cookies, uses a cross-site identifier, or stores your IP address. There's no way for us to single you out in them, which is why there's no cookie banner.

Lawful basis

Aggregate, cookieless counting on a public site: legitimate interest, GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Knowing roughly how many people find the company, and from where, is proportionate, and it can't identify anyone.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you from this website. Nothing is stored beyond the aggregate counts and whatever the providers above keep in their own logs, so there's nothing of yours for us to export or delete. If you think we've got this wrong, you can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate or your national authority.
